Carbon Compensation Service Terms

Version 1.0 · Effective November 13, 2026 · English-language mirror of the Brazilian Portuguese terms.

These terms explain Carbon Pocket's estimated-emissions and compensation service. Compensation does not reduce a user's emissions; they still occur.

1. Definitions

1.1 Carbon credit: a unit representing one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e) reduced, avoided, or removed by a certified project under its applicable methodology.

1.2 Emissions compensation (offset): the acquisition and retirement of certified carbon credits on behalf of contributors to compensate estimated emissions. Compensation does not reduce, avoid, or eliminate the user's own emissions; it funds an equivalent non-emission or removal elsewhere.

1.3 Neutralization and carbon neutrality: a condition that presupposes, in order, inventory quantification, reduction of an organization's own emissions, and compensation of residual emissions only, preferably through removals, under ISO 14068-1. Carbon Pocket does not offer, perform, or attest to neutralization or carbon neutrality.

1.4 Contributor: a user who purchases emissions compensation through the app.

1.5 Retirement batch: credits acquired in a cycle and retired in one public-registry transaction identified by a transaction ID and serial-number range.

1.6 Public registry: an independent platform recording the issuance, transfer, and retirement of carbon credits and providing traceability, such as the Verra registry.

1.7 Public compensation ledger: Carbon Pocket's public page listing, by cycle, tCO2e attributed to contributors and the reference for the corresponding retirement transaction, for third-party verification.

1.8 Footprint estimate: an informational result based on user-provided data and public emission factors. It is not an official inventory and is not intended to meet regulatory, tax, or corporate-reporting obligations.

1.9 CRVE: a Verified Emissions Reduction or Removal Certificate under Brazil's Emissions Trading System (Law 15.042/2024). Credits purchased through Carbon Pocket are not CRVEs.

2. Service scope

Carbon Pocket provides personal emissions estimates, reduction recommendations, and compensation options. Results are informational estimates based on user input and published emission factors; they are not official inventories or a means of meeting regulatory, tax, or corporate-reporting obligations.

3. How compensation works

3.1 Calculation: the footprint estimate uses user-provided data and public, up-to-date emission factors. It is an estimate, not an official inventory.

3.2 Credit selection: Carbon Pocket acquires Verra VCS-certified REDD+ credits (avoided deforestation). The project, identifier, methodology, and vintage are disclosed before purchase. REDD+ credits represent avoided emissions; they are not atmospheric removals.

3.3 Retirement: credits are retired in cycle-based batches in the collective name of that cycle's contributors, in a public registry. The transaction ID, serial range, and retirement date are disclosed. Each serial is retired once and cannot be resold.

3.4 Public allocation: the tCO2e quantity attributed to each contributor is published in the public compensation ledger by cycle.

3.5 Transparency: users can consult the calculation methodology, supported projects, and retirement status of acquired credits at any time.

3.6 Service limits: compensation does not reduce the user's emissions; it creates no right to the credit, which is retired and taken out of circulation; it is not a CRVE; it creates no corresponding adjustment for government NDC accounting; and it is not suitable to substantiate absolute environmental claims.

3.7 Reversal risk: REDD+ projects are subject to reversal risk, mitigated by the certification standard's buffer reserve. Carbon Pocket does not guarantee a climate outcome beyond retirement of acquired credits.

4. Plans, prices, and payment

Plans, prices, volumes, and billing periods are shown before purchase confirmation. Payments are processed by providers identified in checkout. Subscription cancellation follows the instructions presented at purchase and applicable rules.

5. Refunds and withdrawal rights

Before a batch is retired: a full refund is available on request at any time. After a batch is retired: a refund is no longer available for the credit already retired, without affecting other remedies under the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code, including Articles 49 and 51. Carbon Pocket does not condition exercise of the right of withdrawal on waiving rights provided by law.

6. User obligations

  • Provide accurate and current information; estimates depend on user input.
  • Do not present estimates as official inventories.
  • Do not use an individual statement as product, service, event, or company certification.

7. Environmental communication and misleading claims

7.1 Commitment: Carbon Pocket aims to provide truthful, accurate, verifiable, and qualified environmental information under Brazil's Consumer Protection Code, Article 36 and Annex U of the Brazilian Advertising Self-Regulation Code, EU Directive 2024/825, the FTC Green Guides, and the CMA Green Claims Code.

7.2 What Carbon Pocket states: the amount of Verra VCS-certified credits acquired and retired in a pooled batch, with the cycle's transaction ID and serial range published.

7.3 What Carbon Pocket does not claim: carbon neutrality, carbon neutral, net zero, zero emissions, 100% compensated, negligible emissions, permanent carbon removal, or endorsement of the app by a certification body.

7.4 Contributor statement: “I compensated X tCO2e with Verra VCS credits retired in a public registry, published at [LEDGER_URL],” always indicating that this is compensation, not reduction.

7.5 Use limits: this service does not prove climate performance for a product, service, event, company, or person; the estimate is not an official inventory and credits cannot meet SBCE obligations.

7.6 Corporate use: an individual statement is not product certification. Corporate reporting requires a separate GHG Protocol inventory and independent verification.

7.7 Language: consumer information for Brazil is provided in Portuguese as required by Article 31 of Brazil's Consumer Protection Code. Other language versions are faithful mirrors with the same scope.
