📊 Data Sources

📊 Fontes de dados

All emission factors used in CarbonPocket are derived from the GHG Protocol country-specific datasets and national GHG inventories submitted to the UNFCCC. The platform covers electricity emission factors for 96 countries and transport emission factors across 8 modes (Car, Bus, Metro/Train, Train, Motorcycle, Flight, Bicycle, Walking) — sourced from peer-reviewed databases and official government submissions.

Todos os fatores de emissão usados no CarbonPocket são derivados dos datasets do GHG Protocol e inventários nacionais submetidos à UNFCCC. A plataforma cobre fatores de emissão de eletricidade para 96 países e transporte em 8 modos — de bases científicas e submissões oficiais.
