1.Definitions

Platform / App: the Carbon Pocket application and website

User: any individual (or legal entity, where applicable) who uses the app and/or purchases neutralization services.

Carbon credit: a unit representing 1 tonne of CO₂ equivalent (tCO₂e) avoided, reduced, or removed by a certified climate mitigation project.

Neutralization (offset): compensation of the user's estimated emissions through the purchase and permanent retirement of certified carbon credits.

Public registry: an independent platform that records the issuance, transfer, and retirement of carbon credits (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard), ensuring traceability.

2.Object and scope of the service

Carbon Pocket offers: (a) the calculation of the individual carbon footprint based on the transportation, diet, energy, and consumption habits informed by the user; (b) recommendations to reduce emissions; and (c) the neutralization of estimated emissions through certified carbon credits.

Calculated values are informational estimates based on recognized methodologies (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and IPCC factors) and do not constitute official inventories nor are they intended to fulfill regulatory, tax, or corporate reporting obligations.

Important: the service does not promise a physical reduction of the user's emissions. What occurs is compensation through carbon credits — the estimated emission still happens, but is "offset" environmentally by retiring credits on the user's behalf.

3.How neutralization works

The process is executed in a traceable and documented manner:

  • Calculation: the footprint is estimated from the data provided by the user and public, up-to-date emission factors.
  • Credit selection: Carbon Pocket only purchases credits from projects certified under internationally recognized standards (e.g., Verra VCS, Gold Standard, or equivalent).
  • Retirement: credits are retired in a public registry on all the apps user's behalf, becoming permanently removed from the market — preventing double counting or resale.
  • Certificate: every year, the app will provide a digital certificate with the serial number of the credits, the source project, and the amount of tCO₂e neutralized by all the app users.
Transparency: the user can consult, at any time, the calculation methodology, the supported projects, and the retirement status of the purchased credits.

7.Environmental claims and anti-greenwashing

Carbon Pocket is committed to truthful, accurate, and verifiable environmental communication, aligned with the main global and local standards, including:

  • Brazil: Consumer Protection Code (Law 8.078/90) and CONAR rules.
  • European Union: Green Claims Directive, Spanish "Ley verde", Consumer Empowerment Directive (2024/825).
  • United States: FTC Green Guides.
  • United Kingdom: CMA Green Claims Code.

Accordingly, the app does not make claims of "zero emissions", "climate neutral", or "100% offset" without documentary evidence. Any neutralization statement will be accompanied by the certificate, the serial number of the credits, and the exact amount of tCO₂e retired, using precise language about what was actually done.

Commitment: we never sell "neutralization" without credits that have actually been purchased and retired. Transparency is the foundation of our relationship with users and the market.
